Theatre where the audience creates the play in real time

What this is

We are a theatre company exploring new forms of audience participation. Our passion is to create immersive experiences and educational programs where people become active participants. Our ambition is to encourage people not just to consume, but to co-create art.

No actors. No spectators. Everyone plays.

Education

A team learns more from two hours inside a story than from a day of slides.

For companies

Each training is built around what your team is working on: deciding together, dealing with failure, listening under pressure. Game masters run the play and a debrief afterwards.

From 2 to 500 people, from one afternoon to two days.

For schools and institutions

In schools we use role-play, game mechanics and interactive projection for subjects that are hard to teach from a textbook: circular economy, identity, social responsibility. Students learn them by acting them out.

FAQ

What is LARP?

Live action role-play. Everyone in the room takes part and builds the story together. There are no actors and no spectators.

Do I need experience?

No. A short workshop at the start eases you into your character and the rules of the evening.

How long does it last?

Most performances run 1.5 to 2 hours. Trainings for teams run from 4 hours to 2 days.

How many people can take part?

It depends on the performance. Team trainings scale from 2 to 500 people.

What should I expect?

A set that puts you inside the story, a few game mechanics, facilitators who guide without directing, and technology where the story needs it. You create a character, meet the others, and find out things about them and yourself that nobody planned.

Can I take photos?

We photograph every performance and share the photos with you afterwards. Phones stay away during the performance so nobody has to step out of the story.

Which languages?

English and French.

Qualia

We come to know things in different ways. But what can only be known by experiencing?

QUALIA is an immersive and participative performance in which the audience is invited to explore the feeling of doing. Each participant delves into a specifically designed space, questioning the importance of first-hand experiences while applying different ways of communication.

A group of strangers tries to share what they perceive, in words and without them, and discovers how far one mind can reach another. Someone else in the room is trying too.

Created and PremieredResidency at La Générale, Paris (2021)
Presented5th Tel Aviv International Theatre Festival (2023)

Encircling

Imagine losing your memory. Who would you ask for the truth and what would they tell you about yourself?

ENCIRCLING is a collective improvisation performance in which the audience is invited to explore identity through the eyes of others. Each participant starts with no memory of who they are and reconstructs their character through fragments of information shared by the people around them.

Over two hours, the audience becomes the writers, directors and actors of a story unfolding in real time. As they create it together, the line between discovering a character and inventing one begins to blur.

CreatedLARP.Moscow Lab (2016)
PremieredThe Day of Norway Festival, Moscow (2017)
PresentedMoscow · Paris · Berlin
RecognitionTop-8 theatrical performances without actors in Russia (Afisha magazine)

Failure

Everyone keeps one failure they never talk about. An old house in Belleville is collecting them.

FAILURE is a participatory theatre play in which the audience is invited to explore our relationship with a subject we rarely talk about. Each person creates a character and enters the living room of an old theatre, once inhabited by a woman who spent her life collecting stories of failure from around the world.

Through images, cards, words and collective storytelling, the audience uncovers what went wrong in their characters’ lives and how it shaped them. As the stories unfold, they explore whether naming what went wrong can become the first step towards something else.

CreatedIESA Arts & Culture, Paris (2018)
Premiered and PresentedThéâtre Les Enfants Terribles, Belleville, Paris (2019)

Inbox

What if your whole life took just one evening? What message would you leave?

INBOX is an open-air, non-stop 8-hour LARP in which a new group enters the space every 15 minutes. The unprepared public is invited to experience an entire lifetime in one hour. Each participant creates a character before they are born and enters a world that greets them with a letter.

From childhood and youth to friendship, love and growing old together, the story carries each character through the different stages of human existence. Along the way, participants explore what remains after we are gone, ending their journey by writing a letter that will become the beginning of someone else.

CreatedLARP.Moscow Lab (2016)
Premiered and PresentedStay Hungry Festival (2016)
