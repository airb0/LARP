What this is

For teams and schools

For organisations

Each training is built around what your team is working on: deciding together, dealing with failure, listening under pressure. Game masters run the play and a debrief afterwards.

From 2 to 500 people, from one afternoon to two days.

For schools and institutions

In schools we use role-play, game mechanics and interactive projection for subjects that are hard to teach from a textbook: circular economy, identity, social responsibility. Students learn them by acting them out.

Questions

What is LARP?

Live action role-play. Everyone in the room takes part and builds the story together. There are no actors and no spectators.

Do I need experience?

No. A short workshop at the start eases you into your character and the rules of the evening.

How long does it last?

Most performances run 1.5 to 2 hours. Trainings for teams run from 4 hours to 2 days.

How many people can take part?

It depends on the performance. Team trainings scale from 2 to 500 people.

What should I expect?

A set that puts you inside the story, a few game mechanics, facilitators who guide without directing, and technology where the story needs it. You create a character, meet the others, and find out things about them and yourself that nobody planned.

Can I take photos?

We photograph every performance and share the photos with you afterwards. Phones stay away during the performance so nobody has to step out of the story.

Which languages?

English and French.

Qualia

Can you ever really convey what you sense to someone else?

Qualia is the philosophers' word for how an experience feels from the inside: the redness of red, the sound of a voice, the weight of a glance. Everyone has them. Nobody can hand them over.

The performance takes that problem seriously. A group of strangers tries to share what they perceive, in words and without them, and discovers how far one mind can reach another. Someone else in the room is trying too.

CreatedResidency at La Générale, Paris
PremiereOpened the 5th Tel Aviv International Theatre Festival, representing France, September 2023

Encircling

Imagine losing your memory. Who would you ask for the truth, and would they tell it?

You start the evening remembering nothing. The people around you say they know who you are, and each of them tells it differently.

From their fragments you build a character, and slowly see the gap between who you think you are and who the others have decided you are.

A performance about identity, seen through the eyes of the people around you.

Failure

Everyone keeps one failure they never talk about. An old house in Belleville is collecting them.

The house belonged to a woman who spent years collecting stories of failure from strangers passing through. She believed that naming what went wrong could be the first step towards something else.

You step inside and invent a character. Through images and words you find out what failure has made of you, and what it could still make possible.

PremiereThéâtre Les Enfants Terribles, Paris, 2019

Inbox

What if your whole life took just one evening? What message would you leave?

A whole life in one evening. You invent a character before they are born, and the world greets them with a letter. From there the story carries them through childhood, youth, friendship and love, into the years when people grow close and the years when they fall silent side by side.

Inbox is a performance about the circle of life, and about what stays behind: the people who remember us, and what we leave in them.

First playedMoscow, 2016
