

### 1. Objective

Create a high-end, minimalist digital environment for **Larp.Paris**, an immersive theatre laboratory. The design must reflect an "architectural" and "intellectual" tone, avoiding typical "game" or "quest" tropes. It should feel like a secret, safe, yet mysterious space.

### 2. Core Visual Principles

- **Palette:** Absolute Monochrome. Pure Black (`#000000`) background and Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) text. No grey tones for the background.
    
- **Geometry:** Radical linearity. **0px border radius** on all elements (buttons, image frames, input fields). No rounded corners.
    
- **Atmosphere:** Mystery, discovery, and "emergence from darkness."
    

### 3. Typography (Based on Proxima Nova)

- **Weight Contrast:** Emulate the logo's logic—pairing **Black/Bold** with **Light/Thin** weights.
    
- **Headlines:** Use high-contrast weights within a single phrase (e.g., **LARP** PARIS).
    
- **Formatting:** Clean Swiss-style grid. Generous whitespace (Black space). Large line-height for readability on dark backgrounds.
    

### 4. Imagery & Visual Assets

- **Photography Style:** High-contrast B&W. Architectural backgrounds (concrete, metal, sharp shadows).
    
- **Subjects:** Macro-details, hands, "traces of presence" rather than posed portraits.
    
- **Texture:** Subtle film grain and motion blur to add a "tactile" and documentary feel.
    
- **The "Qualia" Exception:** The only use of color is through soft, out-of-focus **bokeh** and light leaks. These should look like "intrusions" into the monochromatic world—vibrant but blurry.
    

### 5. Interaction & Motion (For Web)

- **Emergence:** Content should "step out of the dark" using smooth opacity transitions (Fade-In) triggered by scrolling.
    
- **Navigation:** Minimalist header. On hover, use sharp color inversion (White background / Black text) for buttons only.
    
- **Responsive:** The design must maintain its "monumental" feel on mobile, with large typography and strictly cropped vertical images.
    

### 6. Philosophy to Visualize

"Theatre without a stage. A story without a script." The visuals should not explain everything; they should invite the user to explore the threshold between safety and risk.
